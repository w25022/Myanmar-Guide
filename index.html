Featured Expedition

⛰️ Type: Mountain Trekking / Alpine Expedition
🧗 Difficulty: Very Challenging

🏔️ Hkakabo Razi Trekking

At 5,881 meters (19,295 ft), Hkakabo Razi is the highest peak in Southeast Asia. Located in the pristine Himalayan borderlands of northern Kachin State, this expedition traverses hanging glaciers, sub-alpine pine wilderness, and remote Rawang tribal settlements.

⚠️ Important Expedition Notice
Hkakabo Razi is a serious high-altitude wilderness expedition. Inexperienced tourists must not attempt dangerous alpine ascents without thorough physical conditioning, certified mountaineering guides, government permits, and specialized survival gear.
